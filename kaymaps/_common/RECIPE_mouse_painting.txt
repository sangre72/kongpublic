=== CURVED CHAINED STROKES (toon brushwork, when NOT flat-RLE) ===

★TOOL-SELECTION JUDGMENT TABLE (choose per layer by its GEOMETRY, like a human artist):
  | layer/feature            | shape nature            | TOOL      | why |
  | big flat sky             | large rectangular field | Rect Fill | one drag fills a huge flat zone, no dots |
  | clouds / round masses    | rounded blobs           | Ellipse   | ellipse = cloud puff in 1 drag; chain 2-3 for a cloud bank |
  | tree-wall / mountain     | angular silhouette      | Polygon   | click the few silhouette vertices → clean straight-edged flat mass |
  | straight bank/road/water edge | straight line      | Line      | one drag = crisp straight edge (brush wobbles) |
  | organic foliage/grass texture | irregular, soft    | Brush     | directional sweeps give organic variation |
  | outline / treeline       | thin continuous contour | Line (or chained Lines) | thin straight segments trace silhouette cleanly, size 4-5 |
  | enclosed region recolor  | already-bounded area    | Bucket    | flood-fill inside closed outline (leaks if open) |
