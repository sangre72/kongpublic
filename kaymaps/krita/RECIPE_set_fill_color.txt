★★★★★ANGLE FORMULA CONFIRMED(a_710, 2026-08-18, 3-point-verified <5° error each): hue_degrees = (180 - click_angle_standard_math) mod 360, where click_angle_standard_math = atan2(-(click_y-center_y), click_x-center_x) in degrees(standard CCW-from-right convention).
