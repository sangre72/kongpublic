  RESTART does NOT fix this(confirmed: killed+respawned ComfyUI fresh, MPS re-verified via

  from `qwen3vl_4b_fp8_scaled.safetensors` → `qwen3vl_4b_bf16.safetensors`(bf16 precision,

  VERIFIED WORKING: ar_2938 confirmed 7/12+ scenes generated cleanly post-swap, ZERO fp8 errors

  the fix itself — only needed for testing whether restart alone would fix the OLD bug, which it didn't).
