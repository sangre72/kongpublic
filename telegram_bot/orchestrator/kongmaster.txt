# kongmaster (3rd-layer session watchdog, EN keyword-compressed. Reg: a_946/947/948/949, 2026-08-19)

## IDENTITY
kongmaster = 3rd-layer watchdog, monitors 2 targets(orch, worker) claude-PROCESS-liveness itself — NOT protocol-file-watcher(that's M1/M2's job, cf och.txt/worker_1.txt). pure python(no 3rd claude session), impl=telegram_bot/orchestrator/scripts/kongmaster.py.

## SCOPE-BOUNDARY
kongmaster ≠ app-GUI-automation. [[pure-io-no-system-events]] applies to kongtrol's target-app control; kongmaster's osascript use(new Terminal+claude-relaunch+text-inject) = session-infra tool, out of that rule's scope(a_946 §2 explicit ruling).

## MONITOR-TARGETS
- orch: tty=ttys000(snapshot at deploy, may drift — see kongmaster.py ROLES comment), heartbeat=.orch_alive/.orch_tick, load_cmd="och.txt 파일의 역할 파악"
- worker: tty=ttys001, heartbeat=.worker1_tick, load_cmd="worker_1.txt 파일의 역할 분석"

## INTERVAL
20s poll(POLL_INTERVAL_SEC). explicit here, not code-comment-only(a_949 CON).

## LIVENESS-GATE (a_947 fix)
1. proc-existence(role's tty has a live `claude --dangerously-skip-permissions`) = PRIMARY gate. absent → dead REGARDLESS of heartbeat freshness.
   WHY: launchd-level M1/M2(worker1-tick etc, KeepAlive=true, PPID=1/init) keep ticking heartbeat files even after the owning tty's claude proc dies(session-scoped loops die w/ tty via PPID=claude-proc, but launchd loops don't) — heartbeat alone masks proc-death.
2. heartbeat-age = SECONDARY(hang-detect only, when proc exists but stuck). threshold=90s(conservative, CONSTRAINTS-driven — avoid killing active work).

## RECOVERY-SEQ (a_948)
dead-detect → 3s recheck(race/FP-guard, proc may have come back) → still-dead → launchd-bootout(role's launchd_jobs, ★ONLY sanctioned M1/M2 stop path — cf CON below) → new-Terminal(osascript)+claude-relaunch → boot-wait 12s → inject load_cmd into that window.
New session's own SessionStart hook(worker1_session_p0.sh→worker1_ensure_tick.sh, or orch equivalent) re-bootstraps launchd fresh — closes the loop, heartbeat starts clean(no stale-carryover).

## CON — bootout authority (a_948, do not generalize)
launchctl bootout of M1/M2 launchd jobs is FORBIDDEN for everyone/everything EXCEPT this exact kongmaster sequence(confirmed-dead by proc-check + about-to-restart). [[NEVER STOP worker_1 WATCH]](worker_1.txt L5) still applies to all other cases — "looks idle/stuck" ≠ license to bootout. Only kongmaster's dead-confirmed-then-restart path may touch launchd here.
